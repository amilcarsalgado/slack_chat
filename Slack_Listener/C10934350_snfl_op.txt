- **Apply KA 000001636 Correlation Rules:**
  - If **OSC/TG span loss increases AND fiber span loss increases** → true physical fiber degradation (connectors, ODF, or fiber path).
  - If **OSC/TG span loss increases BUT fiber span loss is stable** → early physical degradation not yet visible to amplified signal (OSC detects it first since it lacks Raman gain).

- **Run `DGN-EQPT`** (TL1 command) against the SRA-41-1 module to check equipment diagnostics.
