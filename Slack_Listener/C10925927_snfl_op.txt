### 3. Known Log/Alarm Signatures to Look For

- `HPM collectPmData call for <NE> was not successful: RTRV-PM-ALL failed: timed out waiting for TL1 response` — TL1 session timeout (msg_id: REST-5)
- `Err: io: read/write on closed pipe` — SSH/SFTP session dropped prematurely (msg_id: PM-118)
- `Err: session timed out` — GNE session timeout (msg_id: PM-118)
- `Status[DENY], Err: <nil>, [::SROF:/*Status, Requested Operation Failed*/]` — NE resource contention
- `Status[DENY], Err: <nil>, [::SARB:/*Status, All Resources Busy*/]` — SSH session limit reached on NE
- `Status[DENY], Err: <nil>, [::IDNV:/*Input, Data Not Valid*/]` — FTP port/configuration issue
- `Failed to validate update threshold message from Kafka: OP must not be empty` — Kafka PM threshold consumer error (msg_id: THRESHOLD-8)
- `DB::Exception: Attempt to read after eof (ATTEMPT_TO_READ_AFTER_EOF)` — ClickHouse inter-node replication failure
- `GCSRefreshException: Query to /gcs/api/v3/config...returned status 503` — GCS service unavailability during Kafka config refresh
